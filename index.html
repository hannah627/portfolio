Publications

Cross-Sector Cybersecurity Performance Goals: Impact

In 2023, I was selected to attend the IT Lab: Summer Security Intensive program at Carnegie Mellon's Heinz College. There, I had the opportunity to collaborate with the Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT) at Carnegie Mellon's Software Engineering Institute (SEI).

My team and I were challenged to enhance feasibility assessments of the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency's (CISA's) Cybersecurity Performance Goals (CPGs) by developing a quantitative method to rate a CPG's impact on an organization.

Our paper describing and justifying our method of calculating a CPG's impact was then peer-reviewed and published as a journal contribution in Carnegie Mellon University's library.
